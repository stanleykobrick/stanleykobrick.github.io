Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

“Kobrick YouTube” is a private application used only by the owner of the Stanley Kobrick YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the owner's consent, the app uses YouTube API Services to read and update the owner's own YouTube channel: videos and their metadata, thumbnails, playlists, comments and analytics reports.

How data is used

Data is used only to manage and analyse the owner's own channel. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is stored on external servers by this app. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
