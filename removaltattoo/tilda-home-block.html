How it works

How laser tattoo removal works

The laser sends very short pulses of light into the skin. The ink absorbs the energy and breaks into tiny particles.

Over the following weeks your body clears those particles away naturally. That's why the tattoo keeps fading between sessions, and why sessions are 6–8 weeks apart.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How many sessions will I need?+

Most tattoos need 5–12 sessions for full removal; fading for a cover-up usually takes 2–4. Small fine-line tattoos can clear much faster, and eyebrow tattoos (microblading) usually need just 1–2 sessions. It depends on the ink, colours, age and your skin. Send a photo for a personal estimate.

Does it hurt?+

Most people compare it to a rubber band snapping on the skin. Sessions are short, and cooling makes it more comfortable.

How long between sessions?+

6–8 weeks. Your body needs time to clear the broken-down ink, which gives better results and protects your skin.

Will it leave a scar?+

With the right settings and good aftercare the risk is low. Following the aftercare matters as much as the session itself.

Can coloured tattoos be removed?+

Black and dark blue respond best. Red, green and light colours can take more sessions, and some may only fade. I'll tell you honestly what to expect.

How much does it cost?+

Tiny tattoos (dots, small birds) from £30 per session, standard tattoos from £60, eyebrow tattoo (microblading) £80 per session. Email or DM a photo on Instagram for an exact quote.
